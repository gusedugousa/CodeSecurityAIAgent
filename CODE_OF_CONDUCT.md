# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, assume good faith, give and accept constructive
feedback, and do not harass or discriminate. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributors that violate these norms. Report concerns through the repository's private
security/contact channels or by contacting the maintainers listed on the repository page.
